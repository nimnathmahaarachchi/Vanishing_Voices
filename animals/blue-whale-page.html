Blue whale estimates cover populations across several oceans.

The blue whale is the largest living animal. It feeds mainly on krill and travels between feeding and breeding areas.

01Distribution and habitat

Blue whales occur in the major oceans. Feeding areas and migration routes differ between populations. The marker indicates a representative location off Chile in the southeast Pacific.

02Threats and population decline

Industrial whaling reduced population numbers substantially. Ship collisions and entanglement remain threats. Underwater noise can affect whale behaviour, while changes in ocean conditions influence food availability.

Ship strikesOcean noiseEntanglement

03Conservation measures

Ship-strike reduction and noise management address continuing threats. Feeding-habitat protection is also relevant. Regional surveys and acoustic observations contribute to population research.
