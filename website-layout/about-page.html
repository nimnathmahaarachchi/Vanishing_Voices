About the project

Vanishing Voices

This educational website presents ten endangered animals. An SVG map links representative habitat locations to individual profiles, which describe distribution and conservation threats.

Project scope

Species information
with referenced estimates

Each profile includes three photographs and a population estimate. The source and evidence date provide context for the figure, although the detail available differs between species.

The atlas is an independent educational project. Conservation organisations retain responsibility for their published estimates, and the selected species represent a small part of threatened biodiversity.

Habitat protection

Habitat loss is discussed in several profiles. Conservation guidance can help explain how land-use decisions affect species survival.

WWF conservation guidance

Wildlife trade

Illegal trade contributes to the decline of several species in this atlas. TRAFFIC publishes research on wildlife trade and related conservation measures.

Wildlife trade information

Population evidence

Published figures should be read alongside their survey dates. Population units also matter because estimates of nesting females differ from counts that include all ages.
