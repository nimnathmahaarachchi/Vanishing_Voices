Data methodology

Population estimates
and sources

Each population figure is linked to a published source. Its unit and evidence date are retained because survey methods differ between species.

01 / Population measure

Counting methods

Sources may count all animals or only adults. The hawksbill figure refers to nesting females, whereas the vaquita figure describes animals observed during a survey. These measures require separate interpretation.

02 / Evidence dates

Survey and publication dates

A publication can refer to an earlier survey. Both dates are displayed where available. Source-check times record access to a page; the date of the population estimate remains attached to its evidence.

03 / Source checks

Verification process

The server searches the linked page for an expected population statement. A failed request leaves the saved estimate visible. Changed figures from fixed reports require editorial review before the baseline is revised.
